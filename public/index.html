Questions

Good to know before you book

Still stuck? Email info@planurstay.com and a real person will help.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You can check out as a guest. Signing in unlocks member prices on hotels and earns points, and you can add a guest booking to your account later with your booking ID and email.

When will I be charged?

You pay the total shown at checkout when you confirm your booking. Some hotels also charge local taxes or resort fees at the property; when they do, we show the amount before you pay.

Can I cancel my booking?

It depends on the rate you choose. Rooms with free cancellation show the deadline before you book. Non-refundable rates and many flight fares can't be refunded.

Where do I find my booking?

Your confirmation is emailed to you and shown on screen. You can also open My trips and look it up with your booking ID and the email you used.

Can I add extras like airport rides?

Yes. When booking a hotel you can add Uber ride credit, paid in the same checkout. Your voucher link comes with your confirmation.
